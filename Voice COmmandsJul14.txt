    Reset ladder (gentle → nuclear):                                                                                
    1. "clear" / "clear effects" — cancels slice + resets orientation, keeps zoom                                   
    2. "reset view" / "reset camera" — full camera reset, keeps layers                                              
    3. "reset" / "whole body" / "start over" / "reset everything" — full nuclear reset to skeleton-only
